This policy describes how Invoice QR Reader (قارئ فاتورة), package `com.poetscorner.zatcaqrcodescanner`, handles data.

## Data handled on your device

With permission, the app uses the camera to read QR codes. Alternatively, it processes an image you select through the system picker. Processing takes place on your device. The app does not record video, save camera frames or retain a copy of the selected image.

The app stores QR content, extracted invoice details, scan times, local check results and invoice classifications in its private local storage. It also stores scanner and appearance preferences. Invoice details may contain names, VAT numbers, amounts and signatures. The current version does not send this information to the developer or a server, does not require an account and operates without an internet connection. It contains no advertising, tracking or usage analytics SDKs.

## Deletion and export

Delete scans from the History screen. Clear the app's data in Android settings or uninstall it to remove its local data. Exporting creates a JSON or CSV file at a destination you choose. That destination may be a cloud provider accessed through the system picker, whose own terms and privacy practices apply. Clearing history or uninstalling the app does not delete exported files; remove them separately at their destination.

## Storage and permissions

Data is kept in the app's private storage. The app does not add its own database encryption layer. It does not request general access to your photos or storage. Camera permission can be declined while continuing to select images. Automatic backup is disabled in the app configuration; device transfer behavior also depends on Android and the device manufacturer.

## Independence and service limits

The app is independent and is not affiliated with or approved by ZATCA. Checks are local and structural, and do not establish official invoice acceptance or the cryptographic authenticity of a signature.
