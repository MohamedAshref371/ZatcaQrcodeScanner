Saudi Invoice QR Reader helps you read Saudi e-invoice QR codes and review the seller, VAT number, total and tax amount. Scan with your camera, choose an image, or paste Base64 text from another app and tap Inspect. Scanning and analysis work offline without an account.

Review invoice details
See the seller name, VAT registration number, invoice timestamp, total including VAT and VAT amount directly. The invoice hash stays with these basic details when phase-two fields are present.

For phase-two invoices, expand Advanced details to view the signature, public key and stamp. This section is hidden for phase-one invoices. Expand the check details or original QR content when needed. Failed local checks are listed for review.

Adjust your scanner
Use the torch, zoom and tap to focus. Choose scan resolution, scan frequency and image-processing options to suit your device and the QR code. Results depend on camera capability and image quality.

Keep a local history
Search previous scans by seller name or VAT number, and tap Search to filter by an inclusive invoice-date or scan-date range. Tap OK to apply your choice or Cancel to keep the current filter. VAT search accepts Arabic and English digits. Open a saved scan, delete an individual entry, or keep only scans from the most recent day you scanned.

Export scans matching your search and applied date filter as JSON or CSV to a location you choose. Clear the search and date range first to export your entire history.

Offline operation
Scanning and analysis work without an internet connection or an account. Scans and preferences stay in the app's local storage. This version includes no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs. Exported files are saved to the destination you select.

Optional developer support
The app is free and ad-free. All invoice features remain free. An optional one-time supporter purchase adds a gold theme and supporter badge. Purchasing and restoring use Google Play and require an internet connection. The local price is displayed before you confirm payment. There is no subscription.

Understand the checks
The app checks data structure, required fields, VAT number format, timestamps and numeric amounts. It labels a scan as a simplified tax invoice when the stamp field is present, or a tax invoice when it is absent. This classification uses the available QR data.

Saudi Invoice QR Reader is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or approved by the Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority (ZATCA). Its checks are local and structural. They do not establish the cryptographic authenticity of a signature or official acceptance of an invoice. The app does not connect to the Fatoora platform.

Supports Arabic and English with the appropriate layout direction, and light and dark themes. Requires Android 8 or later. Camera permission is requested for live scanning. You can also select an image through the system picker without granting access to your entire photo library.
